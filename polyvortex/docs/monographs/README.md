## The dossier anatomy

Every dossier is a self-contained publication: the statement, the proof,
the protocol binding (which rung of the W-ladder certifies it and with
which recorded residuals), the embedded figures relevant to it, and the
cross-references to the sibling statements, to the big monograph
([`../monograph/`](../monograph/)) and to the parent roadmap items the
statement feeds (T1, T3, T5). The dossiers quote only protocol-bound
numbers.

## Which language and format to take

The RU and EN renditions are **separate files**, each complete and
citable on its own. The DOCX is the editable master, the PDF the
typeset render; both carry the same content and the same figures at
300 dpi.

## Formalization queue

The statements feed the mini-repo roadmap items **P1–P3** (see the
[main README](../../README.md#15-roadmap-of-the-mini-repository)): P1
targets Theorem B (the cheapest decidable boundary), P2 the
roots-of-unity identity behind Theorem A plus the chord
pair-cancellation of Lemma C, P3 the integral identity of Lemma D. The
numeric protocols are the oracles those proofs must agree with.
