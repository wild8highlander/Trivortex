## What is inside

The big monograph carries the whole W-ladder program in one volume: the
two layers of the model (Layer K numeric, Layer G analytic) and the
question of where they touch; the five statements — Theorem A (the rigid
rotation generalized to $N$-gon), Theorem B (the admissibility threshold
$\pi\ln 2$), Theorem E (the Havelock stability threshold), Lemma C (the
kinematic obstruction) and Lemma D (the averaged-frequency bridge, the
D1 curve) — with statements, proofs and protocol bindings; the W1–W7
ladder with its registered tolerance bands; all four figures embedded at
300 dpi; the relation map to the parent framework's registers V1–V4 and
roadmap items T1/T3/T5; and the honesty chapter that keeps H1 honestly
obstructed.

## The quoting rule

Every number printed in the monograph originates from the committed
protocols in [`../../results/protocols/`](../../results/protocols/).
The monograph quotes the protocols verbatim — it never recomputes,
approximates or rounds beyond the printed precision of its sources.
