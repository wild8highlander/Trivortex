## The dossier anatomy

Every dossier is a self-contained publication: the statement, the proof,
the protocol binding (which rung of the W-ladder certifies it and with
which recorded residuals), the embedded figures relevant to it, and the
cross-references to the sibling theorems and to the big monograph
([`../monograph/`](../monograph/)). The dossiers quote only
protocol-bound numbers, in the same discipline as everything else in the
mini-research.

## Which language and format to take

The RU and EN renditions are **separate files**, each complete and
citable on its own — there is no single bilingual volume. The DOCX is
the editable master, the PDF the typeset render; both carry the same
content and the same figures at 300 dpi.

## Formalization queue

The theorems feed the mini-research roadmap items **C1–C3** (see the
[main README](../../README.md#15-roadmap-of-the-mini-research)): C1
targets Theorem 1 (pure algebra — the cheapest decidable object), C2 the
reflection register of Theorem 2, C3 the transport identity of Theorem
4. The numeric protocols are the oracles those proofs must agree with.
