## What is inside

Ten sections and the hardcore appendix: the scope and provenance of
the bench; the two layers (the Newtonian planetary dynamics and the
PSL(2,7) figure); the three theorems and two lemmas with proofs —
Theorem A (the exact dimensions of the figure), Theorem B (the seven
equal cells), Theorem C (the rigid heptagon lattice), Lemma D (the
Kepler register and the mass correction), Lemma E (the mass-ladder
deficit); the seven-stage P-ladder; the numerical protocol summary
(P1–P7 and X1–X6); the honesty notes; **Appendix X — the hardcore
register** (the six adversarial stages: the brute-force PSL(2,7), the
two actions, the (2,3,7) generation and the Hurwitz arithmetic, the
hyperbolic {7,3} figure with its disk witness, the integrator
certification, the 1PN perihelion bridge); and a Russian summary
inside the EN volume.

## The quoting rule

Every number in the monograph is bound to a committed protocol in
[`../../results/protocols/`](../../results/protocols/) — the tables
quote the recorded values, the proofs reference the registered
tolerances, the figures are the protocol-bound factory's output. The
monograph never introduces a number of its own.

## Regeneration

The Markdown files are the sources of record; the DOCX renditions are
produced by the parent repository's pandoc pipeline (navy-gold
design), the PDF renditions are the LibreOffice render of the same
DOCX files. The Markdown files are authoritative — regenerate the
renditions from them, never the other way round.
