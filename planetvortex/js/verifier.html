3 · What am I looking at?

The Klein quartic x³y + y³z + z³x = 0 is the genus-3 surface whose symmetry group PSL(2,7) — 168 elements, the smallest non-abelian simple group — reaches the Hurwitz bound 84(g−1). Its {7,3} tessellation has 24 heptagons glued in antipodal pairs: 12 gravitational registers. The mass ladder of the solar system — the Sun, the eight planets, Ceres, Pluto, Eris — is carried by the vertex angles αi = 2π/3 − σ·si with si = ln GMi − ⟨ln GM⟩; the geometric-mean normalization makes Σsi = 0 exactly, and that is precisely the condition that the decorated tessellation closes on the Gauss–Bonnet budget Σαi = 8π = 2π(2g−2). The Euler characteristic absorbs the mass ladder. The heavier the body, the wider its heptagon — gravity drawn as geometry, in exact closed form: cosh R = cot(α/2)cot(π/7), cosh(ℓ/2) = cos(π/7)/sin(α/2), cosh r = cos(α/2)/sin(π/7), A = 5π − 7α.
